Building a Balanced Plate

Why Balance Matters at Every Meal

A balanced plate fuels your body for studying, playing, thinking, and growing. Aim to fill it with colourful fruit, fresh vegetables, whole grains, and a good source of protein.

Learn more about balanced eating here: MyPlate Nutrition Guide

Smarter Snack Picks

Snacking can be healthy when you reach for foods that give lasting energy instead of just sugar. Fruit, nuts, sprouts, and yoghurt beat chips and sugary drinks every time.

Explore more nutrition facts here: WHO Healthy Diet Guide

Wellness Tips for Students

Stay Hydrated

Water keeps your body active, supports digestion, and helps your brain stay sharp. Carry a water bottle and sip throughout the day.

Eat the Rainbow

Different colours in fruits and vegetables bring different nutrients to the table. A colourful plate is usually a well-rounded plate.

Read more about smart food choices here: Nutrition.gov

Quick Nutrition Note

Try this simple rule: fill half your plate with fruits and vegetables, one quarter with whole grains, and one quarter with protein.
