Bob

Enter Bob, a stocky British Bulldog with a scarred muzzle and a heart of pure gold, who serves as the syndicate’s chief safe-cracker and heavy operations specialist. Before Buddy found him, Bob was trapped in the grim, underground world of illegal guard-dog rings, chained up in a rusty junkyard and forced to be mean. Buddy and the crew pulled off their most daring heist yet to break him out, blowing the facility's gates and cutting Bob's heavy chains. Now officially rescued and sporting a sharp, pinstriped mobster suit that struggles to contain his broad shoulders, Bob has traded his life of neglect for a seat at the highest table in the criminal underworld.

About Bob

Despite his intimidating, heavyweight boxer physique and a permanent underbite that makes him look like a classic 1920s enforcer, Bob is actually the emotional anchor of the crew. He is fiercely loyal to Buddy for giving him a second chance at life, viewing the gang not as a criminal enterprise, but as the first real family he has ever known. While Chico runs around panicking about police response times, Bob sits like an unmovable boulder, calmly chewing on a cigar stub and cracking safes with a specialized stethoscope pressed against his floppy ears. He treats every bank vault like a puzzle, his stubby tail giving a rare, joyful wag every time a heavy steel door clicks open.

Bob’s rescued status gives him a unique advantage when it comes to outsmarting the authorities. Because he still looks like a slightly battered, slow-moving stray to the untrained eye, he often plays the role of the innocent bystander during a getaway. While Buddy and Bruno tear down the street in a cloud of tire smoke, Bob will casually waddle past the arriving police cruisers, wearing a pathetic, droopy-faced expression that immediately makes the officers stop to offer him a treat and scratch his ears. By the time the cops realize the "poor, lost bulldog" was actually carrying three million dollars worth of diamonds in his oversized collar pouch, Bob has already boarded a city bus, heading back to HQ.

At the end of a long day of breaking into high-security vaults, Bob’s favorite pastime is enjoying the simple comforts of a rescued life that he was denied for so long. In the back of their butcher-shop hideout, right next to the tables piled high with stolen cash, Bob has a massive, orthopedic memory-foam bed where he snores loud enough to rattle the windows. He spends his cut of the loot on the finest orthopedic care for older dogs and anonymous donations to the shelter workers who helped him survive his darkest days. He might be a wanted felon by day, but by night, Bob is just a grateful dog who finally knows what it feels like to be safe, warm, and part of a pack.
