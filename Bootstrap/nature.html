Rafflesia Arnoldii

Rafflesia arnoldii is an extraordinary parasitic plant native to the rainforests of Sumatra and Borneo. Famous for producing the world’s single largest individual flower, this "corpse flower" lacks leaves, stems, or roots and emits a putrid stench of rotting meat to attract pollinating insects
